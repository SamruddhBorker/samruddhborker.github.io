FAQ

Questions owners ask me.

Do I need to write anything?

No. I visit, take the photos and write the text with you, in English or in your language. You check everything before it goes live.

How do enquiries reach me?

A customer fills in a short form on your site, and WhatsApp opens with their message already written. They press send, and it arrives in your WhatsApp like any other chat.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain and hosting are in your name, and I hand over every login once the final payment is made.

What do the domain and hosting cost?

You pay the providers directly, not me. A domain is usually around ₹1,000 a year, and a site like this can run on very low-cost or even free hosting. I'll recommend the simplest option for you.

What if I need changes later?

Website care starts at ₹999 a month and covers updates, backups and small changes. You can also pay for one-off changes when you need them.

Will I come first on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. I set up the basics properly and connect your site to your Google listing, so customers who search for you can find you.

Where do you work?

I'm based in Ponda, and I mostly visit businesses in Ponda, Margao, Old Goa and nearby. If you're further away, ask anyway.
