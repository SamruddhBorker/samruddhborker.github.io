What we serve

Made fresh, every morning

  • Specialty coffee

    Single-origin beans from Chikmagalur, pulled as espresso, pour-over or our signature coconut cold brew.

  • Goan breakfasts

    Poi with chorizo, choris pão, bhaji-puri and egg dishes, plus vegan bowls with local fruit.

  • Fresh bakes

    Bebinca, coconut cookies and sourdough baked in-house daily. Pre-order cakes for celebrations.

  • Private events

    Sunset birthdays, small weddings and team breakfasts for up to 40 guests on our garden deck.

Our story

A little café by the sea

We opened Coastal Café in an old Portuguese-era house a short walk from Candolim Beach. Our idea was simple: good coffee, honest Goan food and a shady verandah where nobody rushes you.

Our ingredients come from local farmers and fishers wherever we can, and our team is mostly from the neighbourhood. Come for breakfast and stay for the breeze.
