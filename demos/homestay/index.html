Our home

Four generations, one mango tree

Casa Mangueira has been in our family for four generations. When we restored the front rooms, we kept what makes the house special: laterite walls, oyster-shell windows and the long balcão where everyone ends up in the evening.

We live downstairs, so breakfast is whatever we're having: poi, bhaji, fruit from the yard and strong coffee. Ask us about Loutolim and we'll point you to the quiet spots.
